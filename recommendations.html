To: To Whom It May Concern
From: Peter Molina, Former Director of Information Systems, HRL Laboratories
Subject: Letter of Recommendation for Nicholas Stafford

Dear Hiring Manager,

It is my pleasure to provide this letter of recommendation for Nicholas Stafford, who served as a Service Desk Technician within the HRL Information Systems department. As the department head, I had the opportunity to witness Nicholas’ professional growth and his contributions to our technical operations.

While at HRL, Nicholas distinguished himself through both his technical skills and his exceptional composure when dealing with high-stakes situations. In our defense contracting environment, we often faced time-sensitive technical hurdles that required a level-headed approach. Nicholas’s ability to remain patient and approachable while solving critical issues made him a favorite among the staff and leadership alike.

Beyond his interpersonal strengths, Nicholas demonstrated a consistent drive for professional development. He was frequently seeking out new challenges and taking the initiative to streamline workflows. Whether he was troubleshooting a difficult end-user issue or finding ways to improve processes, he approached every task with a focus on efficiency and long-term value.

I have no doubt that Nicholas’ combination of continuously increasing technical abilities, people skills, and outstanding character will make him an asset to any team. He has my highest recommendation for any role that requires a blend of technical competency and superior people skills.

Should you require any further information regarding Nicholas’ performance or qualifications, please do not hesitate to contact me.

Sincerely,
